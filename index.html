Healthy Start

Simple PMTCT information and help finding services

Are you pregnant or caring for a new baby?

You can get information about HIV testing, treatment and care for you and your baby.

You are not alone.
PMTCT services can help protect the health of mothers and babies.

What do you need help with?

Important

Your HIV result is confidential. This guide does not ask for your name, HIV status or medical record.

🩺 I want an HIV test

When should I test?+
Ask for an HIV test at your first antenatal visit. A health worker may recommend another test later in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
Will my result be private?+
HIV testing and results should be handled confidentially. Ask the health worker if you have concerns.
What if my test is positive?+
A health worker can explain your result and help you start the care and treatment you need.
Can my partner test too?+
Yes. Ask the health worker about partner testing and support.

💊 I have HIV / I want treatment

What should I do?+
Talk to a health worker about starting or continuing HIV treatment (ART) as soon as possible.
Can treatment protect my baby?+
Taking HIV treatment as prescribed can greatly reduce the risk of HIV passing from mother to baby.
What about my baby after birth?+
Ask the health worker about medicine for your baby and when your baby should have HIV testing.

👶 I need help for my baby

Will my baby need medicine?+
If the mother has HIV, the baby may need medicine soon after birth. Ask a health worker what is recommended.
When should my baby be tested?+
Ask the health worker about the recommended HIV testing schedule for your baby.
How should I feed my baby?+
Ask your health worker about the safest feeding option for you and your baby while following your HIV treatment.

📍 Where can I get help?

Look for a health facility that provides antenatal care and HIV services.

Open Health Facility Finder
Before you go
Ask whether the facility provides HIV testing, HIV treatment (ART), antenatal care and services for babies.

If you cannot find a facility

Ask at your nearest health centre or antenatal clinic where you can get PMTCT services.

Facility information should be checked before relying on it. This prototype does not claim that every listed facility provides every PMTCT service.
